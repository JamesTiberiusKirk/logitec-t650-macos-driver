--- CORRECTION (appended after review) ---
The line above printing "origin = 1  (1 = upper-left)" is WRONG. The annotation
in the script was incorrect; the captured byte is right.
Per hid-logitech-hidpp.c:
    TOUCHPAD_RAW_XY_ORIGIN_LOWER_LEFT  0x01
    TOUCHPAD_RAW_XY_ORIGIN_UPPER_LEFT  0x03
origin == 1 therefore means LOWER_LEFT, so the kernel sets flip_y and reports
y_out = y_size - y_raw. See T650_PROTOCOL_SPEC.md section 3.4 / 5.8.
